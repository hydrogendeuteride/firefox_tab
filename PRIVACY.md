# Privacy

Window Tabs does not send tab lists, imported files, settings, or usage data to its developer or analytics services. It has no remote server, advertising, or telemetry.

- Tab titles, URLs, and pinned status are processed locally for export and import. Imported websites load normally and receive the usual web requests.
- Settings and the latest auto-save result, including a filename or error, are stored locally.
- Auto-save keeps regular-window tab snapshots in session storage until disabled or Firefox exits. Private windows are excluded.
- Exported files remain until you delete them, including after uninstalling the extension.

Permissions: `tabs` reads tab information, `downloads` saves files, and `storage` keeps settings and snapshots.
